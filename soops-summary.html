SOOPs summary - Solar Orbiter

Long Term Planning and Remote-Sensing Windows information

 

A description of the SOOP summary table

  • The first column lists the generic description of the Solar Orbiter Observing Plan (SOOP) without the mnemonic system (see this explanation on the SOOPs for further information)
  • The second column provides more information on the intended observation during the run of the SOOP
  • Columns 3-7 + Column 12 provide further information on the SOOPs
  • Columns 8-11 provide information on coordinated observations during that time, to the best knowledge of the project scientists
  • Columns 13-14 provide information on where to get the data from the archive (Column 13: N/A yet) and quicklooks from the instruments over the period covered by the SOOP
  • Column 15 provide further notes on the SOOP such as information on the target, papers published etc. 

 

How to use the SOOP summary table

  • Columns can be hidden/shown for further ease of investigation
  • If keywords are known (e.g. SOOP names, Long-Term Planning name), the top search bar allows to search through the whole table
  • SOOPs can be listed per column (by default: SOOPs are listed by their start date)

  • SOOP data can be explored by clicking on the "Observation summary" links provided in the column "Quicklooks". This provides a brief summary of the location of Solar Orbiter during the SOOP duration ("Orbit Plot"), the data availability in the Solar Orbiter Archive, the Fields-of-View of EUI/HRI, SPICE and PHI/HRT (whenever available), as well as a list of images and plots from all the instruments that were observing during that time.

 

Call to the science community: please provide feedback (including any science inputs relevant to the observations) for the SOOP summary table at the link here.
